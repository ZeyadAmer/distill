Your clipboard, refined. — macOS App Support

How to use Hotstash

  1. Launch the app — a flame icon appears in the menu bar.
  2. Copy anything as usual with ⌘C.
  3. Press your global hotkey (set in Settings) or click the menu bar icon to open your clipboard history.
  4. Use arrow keys or mouse to browse. Press Return to copy the selected item.
  5. Switch to your target app and press ⌘V to paste.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't the app appear after launch?

Hotstash is a menu bar app — it has no dock icon. Look for the flame icon in the top-right menu bar.

How do I change the hotkey?

Click the gear icon in the clipboard panel, or right-click the menu bar icon and choose Settings.

How do I unlock Hotstash Pro?

Open the clipboard panel. When the trial expires, a banner appears at the bottom with an "Unlock Hotstash" button. Tap it to complete the in-app purchase.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open Settings → tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Where is my clipboard data stored?

All clipboard history is stored locally on your Mac. Nothing is sent to any server.

How do I clear my clipboard history?

Click the trash icon in the clipboard panel toolbar to clear all non-pinned items.
